HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTOGRAPHS 📸
==================================

1. Copy your personal photos (e.g. JPG, PNG, WEBP) directly into this folder:
   f:\vanshu-little-world\public\images\

2. For example, name them:
   - vanshu-1.jpg
   - vanshu-2.jpg
   - first-date.jpg

3. In your website, set the image path to:
   /images/vanshu-1.jpg

4. You can update the photo list either:
   - By editing src/data/content.js
   - Or by visiting http://localhost:3000/admin and entering the image path!

When deployed to Vercel or Netlify, all photos placed in public/images/
are automatically bundled and publicly accessible on your website!
